# Akuafodamfo — Android App for Ghanaian Farmers

Akuafodamfo (Twi: "Farmer's Friend") is an offline-first Android app that empowers Ghanaian smallholder farmers to:
- Detect crop diseases early using on-device AI (cassava, maize, cocoa, yam)
  <img width="576" height="1280" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-06-01 at 2 49 58 AM (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e6dcf28f-d327-41e9-b96b-6c3de3931304" />

  
- Optimize planting decisions via voice-guided recommendations on soil, rainfall & planting calendar
  <img width="576" height="1280" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-06-01 at 2 49 58 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a54d63ef-8f9b-43b5-844e-6fc663fc24d8" />

- Secure better market prices with real-time regional price alerts

🌍 Why This Matters
Over 60% of Ghanaian farmers have limited literacy. Akuafodamfo solves this with **full Twi voice interaction** — no reading or writing required. Farmers speak in their native language, and the app responds audibly.

 📱 Key Features
- 🗣️ Twi Voice Interface 
- 📸 Offline Disease Detection (TensorFlow Lite)
- 🌱 Planting Decision Support (localized to Ghana's agro-ecological zones)
- 💰 Voice-Based Market Price Alerts
- 📶 Offline-First — works without internet, syncs when available

 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Android (min API 24) + Kotlin
- TensorFlow Lite (quantized EfficientNet-Lite)
- Firebase (optional sync, auth)
- Twi ASR/TTS (custom + Google Speech-to-Text)

 📊 Impact Targets
- 35% reduction in crop losses
- 20% increase in farmgate prices for users
- 10,000+ daily active voice users within 1 year

 🤝 Open Source & Community
Built for Ghanaian farmers. Contributions, field testers, and Twi voice data donations welcome.
